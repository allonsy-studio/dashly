Metadata, feeds, & absolute URLs

Set a base URL once

absoluteUrl resolves paths against it. Full URLs, mailto:, tel:, & fragment links pass through unchanged. Without baseUrl, paths come back as given, which keeps local development simple.

Canonical & social tags

Dates for machines & people

toISOString feeds <time> elements & JSON-LD. longDate & timeAgo read well on the page.

Date-only strings such as 2026-01-15 render as the same day everywhere: formatting defaults to UTC.

Feeds

cleanForRSS strips scripts, embeds, & heading permalinks from rendered HTML. Pass customTags for web components that feed readers cannot render. It needs the posthtml peer.
